About MissVoice

Our Mission

MissVoice bridges communication gaps between deaf, mute and hearing people using Artificial Intelligence.

AI Translation

Our AI model detects hand gestures, recognizes sign language, converts them into text and speaks them aloud.

Future Goal

Support live video calling, real-time multilingual sign language, and global communication.

Features

📷 Live Camera

🤖 AI Recognition

🔊 Voice Output

Real-time gesture prediction within milliseconds.
